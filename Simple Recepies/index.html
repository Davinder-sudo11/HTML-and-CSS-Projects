Healthy Vegetable Bowl

Preparation: 15 minutes

A fresh and healthy meal made with vegetables.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup mixed vegetables
  • 1 cup cooked rice
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • Salt and pepper

Instructions

Cook the vegetables in olive oil with salt and pepper. Serve them over cooked rice.

Fresh Garden Salad

Preparation: 10 minutes

A colorful salad that is quick and easy to make.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups lettuce
  • 1 tomato
  • 1 cucumber
  • Lemon juice and olive oil

Instructions

Wash and chop the vegetables. Mix them in a bowl, add lemon juice and olive oil, and serve fresh.

Sweet Homemade Treat

Preparation: 20 minutes

A delicious sweet treat for special moments.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder

Instructions

Mix the dry ingredients, add milk, and stir into a smooth batter. Bake in a suitable prepared pan until cooked through.

About Simple Recipes

Simple Recipes was created for people who enjoy homemade food but do not always have a lot of time to cook. Our goal is to make cooking easy, enjoyable, and accessible to everyone.

This website shares beginner-friendly recipes with simple ingredients and easy-to-follow instructions. Whether you are learning to cook or looking for ideas for your next meal, you can find inspiration here.

Our Mission

Our mission is to encourage people to prepare fresh meals, try new dishes, and build confidence in the kitchen. We believe that good food does not need to be complicated.
